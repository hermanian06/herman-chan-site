Model-ready outputs

157 fields the pipeline extracts from a deal's raw documents and hands to your model. It does not build a model — every shop already has one, and theirs is the one their committee trusts. The work worth automating is filling it in correctly. Comps come only from an operator's own weekly rent scrape; licensed sources are excluded by design, so a field one of them would have filled reads MISSING rather than being sourced elsewhere.

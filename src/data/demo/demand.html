Demand

Ring demographics from the operator's own engine, via the market-report service. ACS 2020-2024 5-year, 2020 Decennial baseline, LODES 2023 workplace counts, BLS metro series. All public, no licensed provider.

Read this before the 1-mile column. The 1-mile column overstates levels. Ring membership is decided by each census block group's internal point, then the whole block group is counted — at 1 mile a single large block group can sit mostly outside the ring and still be counted whole. Measured against a commercial source, 1-mile population reads roughly 2.3x and households roughly 1.9x too high. The 3-, 5- and 10-mile columns do not have this problem. Read the 1-mile column for MIX and SHARES (income distribution, renter share, sector concentration), which are ratios and survive the overcount, and read levels off the 3-mile column.
